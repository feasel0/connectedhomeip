## 4. Confidence and Wording

There is strong direct history evidence for **when these exact faulty setups entered master**: October 28 at 06:39 UTC in #74722. There is also actual CI evidence that the introducing revision initially passed, followed by failures on later runs. No new runtime bisect, controlled delayed-response experiment or CI retry was performed. The source addition and unchanged setup contract support attribution without treating an intermittent runtime observation as a deterministic bisect result.

Earlier use of "preexisting upstream defect" meant preexisting relative to the Closure Dimension follow-ups, not a claim that this defect had existed on master for weeks. This history supports the more precise description: **recently introduced intermittent Darwin test-setup failure, independent of the Closure Dimension changes and unrelated to IDM coverage discovery**.
