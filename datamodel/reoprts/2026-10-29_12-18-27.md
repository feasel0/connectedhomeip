## Findings And Caveats

### 72823: Deferred CASE Cleanup

1. Copilot high: a queued release lambda can outlive the CASE client pool during controller shutdown; pending POSIX device-event work may later call through a dangling pool pointer after the client was already destroyed. The review requests lifetime coordination or cancellation before pool destruction. Comment: `https://github.com/project-chip/connectedhomeip/pull/72823#discussion_r4411946641`.
2. Copilot medium and CodeRabbit minor: TIMEOUT/BUSY retries call the next-address path synchronously before deferred release frees the old client. At pool capacity, alternate-address allocations can fail with NO_MEMORY without a handshake. The reviewers request release/retry ordering that also preserves callback, setup and cancellation lifetimes; Copilot asks a pool-at-capacity regression. Comments: `https://github.com/project-chip/connectedhomeip/pull/72823#discussion_r4411946709` and `https://github.com/project-chip/connectedhomeip/pull/72823#discussion_r4412012573`.

The snapshot has twelve unresolved #72823 threads: these three new bot threads plus nine pre-existing human threads, including one outdated thread. They were not resolved or treated as twelve new bot defects. Copilot marks its old event-loop-stop finding resolved. These are reported review findings, not new locally reproduced failures.

### 74889: Linux EAP Wire Contract And Aliases

1. CodeRabbit major: generic WPA-EAP is mapped to the generated PDC-named bit without establishing the intended wire contract. It requests alignment of both production decoders with the advertised contract. Fresh open thread: `https://github.com/project-chip/connectedhomeip/pull/74889#discussion_r4412045081`. Copilot's existing current-head review likewise requests advertised-version confirmation; its linked original second-decoder thread was already resolved.
2. Three collapsed additional comments in review `5697223711` must not be omitted: two report missing `WPA-EAP-SUITE-B-192` recognition, one for each decoder's parser, which could leave an EAP-only BSS classified as open; the third warns about using generic EAP under an older PDC-only contract. CodeRabbit explicitly labels all three unverified and explicitly says the two alias omissions predate this PR. Review: `https://github.com/project-chip/connectedhomeip/pull/74889#pullrequestreview-5697223711`.

The contract objection is version-sensitive; earlier qualification targeted the current in-progress Matter 1.7 definition and retained an older-contract/backport caveat. This audit does not adjudicate that specification question or establish the unverified alias observations as newly introduced regressions.
