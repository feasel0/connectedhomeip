The authenticated current specification master remains
`44f620f218fec98537b83f3f236834c584a0d26d`; the Network Commissioning chapter blob
`7a75e1f9755def0c73c2f4201c5e8f2f8ecec6d5` and SHA-256

5 as the broader IEEE8021X category, while the SDK's generated model retains the
older PDC-only definition/name. Version 1.7/revision 3 alone cannot distinguish
the provisional interpretations. Raw private specification prose is not published.

in-progress target, obtain an explicit applicability decision, and separately
align the stale generated model/public naming. If older PDC-only consumers must
remain compatible, agree a legacy scan-result policy before changing either
decoder. Do not silently drop generic EAP, invent a reserved bit, or infer PDC
capability from arbitrary EAP membership. The pre-existing Suite-B parser omission
also remains dependent on this choice and was not changed.

**Decision for the user:** should this PR target the current in-progress
IEEE8021X contract only, or must it preserve older PDC-only consumers?
