readiness milestone. They do not prove that every broader design/resource/test
validity concern is fixed or that the user has accepted every remaining caveat.
The recorded seven-ready/one-CI-blocked result must not be used as that claim.

For the three remaining concerns, the recorded recommendation is to keep the
current PRs narrowly scoped and use separate changes: bounded Push AV duplicate
checking preserving status/callback behavior; IDM baseline-validity containment
and explicitly configured safe command setup; and Step validation before effects
with an explicit endpoint/clamping policy. These are proposals, not already-opened
or implemented follow-up PRs. If any is required by the user's acceptance criteria
for the current PRs, that requirement remains outstanding rather than satisfied
by technical readiness. No new acceptance decision is inferred here.
