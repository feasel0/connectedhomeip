### Impact

Packaged-model command entries without their own conformance now inherit the base rule instead of defaulting to Optional:

- RVC Operational State (1.3 and later): Pause, Resume and OperationalCommandResponse.
- Oven Cavity Operational State (1.4 and later): Stop and OperationalCommandResponse.
- Thermostat Mode (1.7): ChangeToMode, ChangeToModeResponse and ChangeToModeByCoreTag. ChangeToMode and ChangeToModeByCoreTag also change their invoke privilege from Operate to Manage.

TC_DeviceConformance and TC_AccessChecker can therefore be stricter for these clusters, and a DUT can receive a different result. This corrects the parsed requirements; it does not change certification policy or DUT runtime behavior.
