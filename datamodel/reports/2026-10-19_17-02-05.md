## Makefile and Include Records

The pinned specification Makefile provides the combined `pdf`/`pdf-book` rule.
It does not provide `pdf-appclusters-book`, `pdf-devicelibrary-book`, or
`pdf-standardnamespaces-book`. The main document includes the application
clusters, device library, and standard namespaces sections.

The actual successful `make PRINT_FILENAMES=1 ... pdf` output contains all three
section entrypoints: `appclusters.adoc`, `device_library.adoc`, and
`standard_namespaces.adoc`. The include processor prints include targets while
normal recursive inclusion continues. Every retained cluster, device type, and
namespace has a corresponding exact include record, except the three historical
label-cluster shared-source exceptions preserved by the existing script.

## Compatibility Fix

- Collect exact include basenames rather than testing substrings of Make output.
- Reuse the combined main-book records only when all three section entrypoints
  are present.
- Otherwise retain all three historical separate-book builds and union the core
  and application-cluster records without concatenating line boundaries.
- Preserve the existing shared-source exceptions, include defines, dry-run and
  pre-1.2 behavior, and propagation of real scraper/Make failures.

Exact membership prevents, for example, excluded `Cluster.xml`, `Type.xml`, and
`space.xml` from being retained merely because unrelated includes end in
`CoreCluster.adoc`, `DeviceType.adoc`, and `Namespace.adoc`.
