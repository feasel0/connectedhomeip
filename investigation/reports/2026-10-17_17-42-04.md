Git-ignore boundary and accidentally exposing tens of gigabytes of evidence or
controller secrets. Versioning the new ignore policy and checking both the old
hidden set and the final staged snapshot addresses that risk directly.

That assumption is incompatible with the requested archival branch, so the
test now verifies visibility rather than permanent dirty status. Historical

interrupting active services. They are intentionally local and ignored. Future
tooling should prefer the new layout, while old evidence paths remain valid on
this host. A fresh clone contains the summaries and tooling but not the excluded
