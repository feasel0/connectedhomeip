Constructive sequence on one already-established LE connection: emit groupA
with identifier7; leave it pending; consume127 identifiers through other
signaling, for example credit replenishments on another established channel;

pending connection channels plus a credit-carrying channel do not exhaust
dynamic CIDs. There is no source-enforced allocation rate or round-trip gate
that makes127 credit/signaling allocations within40 seconds impossible.
Connection locking serializes these operations, but does not keep the mutex
held while awaiting A's remote response.

channels. This does not establish that the deployed BlueZ EATT workload
actually generates it; the original six have no observed identifier collision.

it does not execute the socket admission, credit transport or timer machinery.
Those parts of reachability are established by the pinned source review.

## 2. Smallest Demonstrated Regression Correction

The previous proposal allowed two unrelated index0 channels with identifier7
to consume one response slot. The second lookup found the first accepted
channel's DCID and reset it through duplicate-DCID handling. Original code
instead consumed the sole slot once, accepted the first and refused the next.

The patch adds one response-local unsigned-long bitmap, initially zero. After
the existing array-length check, test_and_set_bit rejects a previously used
transmit index with the existing ECONNREFUSED path. Each emitted index is0..4,
so it fits the bitmap on both32/64-bit targets. It adds no channel field,
allocation, list movement or global-allocator policy beyond the original
isolated index proposal. The bounds/bit test occurs before reading the DCID
or performing duplicate-DCID lookup. A removed channel never consumes a slot;
distinct slots containing the same DCID still reach the unchanged duplicate
policy and discard both channels as before.

This addresses the demonstrated new collateral deletion, not the inherited
ambiguity. First traversal matching a slot still wins; another unrelated
pending request may still be refused or incorrectly associated when identifiers
collide. No claim of full transaction isolation. Such weakness must not be
misrepresented as the historical CID-ordering defect or an automatic blocker
requiring a global allocator redesign before the original correction proceeds.
