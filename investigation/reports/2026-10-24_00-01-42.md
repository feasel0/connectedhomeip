Report date: 2026-10-24 UTC. Continuation of investigation HEAD

## Conclusions

The replacement's long DUT revisit is substantially explained: 15 concurrent
subscription handlers share four reporting credits. Successful confirmation
releases one credit; the engine promptly spends it at the forward-moving cursor,
not necessarily on the confirming handler. Twelve runs intervene for each target.
The cursor and competing lifetimes account for the target not being visited for
7.263-10.227 seconds. This is not evidence of a lost wakeup or a target repeatedly
visited but denied service. It does not establish a universally adequate timeout
or prove every unrecorded scheduler predicate throughout the interval.

There is a separate, measured controller boundary. Each exact next ReportData is
captured before its actual native read timeout, but its first matching `recvmsg`
occurs afterward. The 0.920-3.321-second capture-to-receive windows contain
identifiable same-thread report callbacks, authenticated-before-dispatch work,
other callbacks, and 25-284 ms outside recorded scopes. They are not uniformly
socket-queue residence or uniformly logging. Internal wall spans do not prove CPU
occupancy or exclude blocking/descheduling within a callback.

A targeted historical kernel receipt establishes hci0 command failures and USB
resets followed by hci1 initialization immediately before the eleven
adapter-unavailable recovery outcomes. This strengthens a historical subset
attribution without identifying the old adapter's MAC or explaining all 66 BLE
outcomes from today's hci1 inventory.

The original counters version has been recovered with an exact original-seal
hash match, and a workload-free exact-path mount-namespace isolation proof passed.
The earlier replacement still was not fully isolated qualification.

**Historical accounting remains 158 FAIL / 80 mapped / 78 OPEN.** No hardware
invocation, BLE scan, service recovery, production SDK change, timeout change,
scheduler change, or administration of separately owned PRs was performed.
