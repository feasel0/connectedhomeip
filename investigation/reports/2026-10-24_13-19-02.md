## Lifecycle and Ledger Contract

SQLite uses WAL, FULL synchronous commits and explicit immediate transactions.
Cell and unit uniqueness prevent duplicate admission. A reserved attempt ID is
committed before launch. Terminal transitions are idempotent. Service ambiguity
becomes INCOMPLETE/BLOCKED, never PASS and never an implicit same-ID rerun.
This prototype deliberately does not admit replacement attempts after ambiguity.

Workers record machine/boot identity, PID/start ticks, systemd invocation ID,
attempt ID and source hash. The coordinator reconciles their durable receipts
with independently supervised service outcomes. Worker lifetime is independent
of coordinator service restart. This is local synthetic reconciliation only;
it is not a qualified remote worker implementation.

The wall-clock start/deadline is committed at first actual coordinator run, not
at manifest preparation. Restart cannot reset it. Synthetic manifests are hashed
and frozen; changed manifest/source is blocked. No approved native matrix was
substituted. Hardware manifests are rejected explicitly.

Restart policy is `Restart=on-failure`, one-second backoff and at most three
starts per 60 seconds. Workers have independent runtime/stop limits and cgroup
cleanup. Coordinator graceful stop preserves interrupted attempts. A separate
watchdog preserves hang diagnostics before stopping the exact owned worker.
Unsafe/ambiguous outcomes block further admissions. Ordinary synthetic FAIL
does not block the next cell.

PASS detail pruning requires a matching hashed receipt already committed in
SQLite. The receipt and accounting remain. FAIL, BLOCKED, interrupted and uncertain
details remain. This is not a validated capture/archive/controller-storage
retention adapter for the future hardware campaign.

authorization or instructions to launch the hardware campaign.

